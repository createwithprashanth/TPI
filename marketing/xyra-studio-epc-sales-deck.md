# XYRA Studio EPC Sales Deck

## Slide 1 - Hero

XYRA Studio

Private AI for EPC drawing deliverables.

Turn P&IDs and PDFs into review-ready engineering outputs.

XYRA helps instrumentation and piping teams extract tags, line context, MTO counts, and QA evidence inside the client network.

## Slide 2 - Why Now

Manual drawing take-off is a capacity problem.

EPC teams need faster first-pass deliverables without pushing client drawings outside the network.

- Manual extraction
- Review drift
- Data restrictions
- Small specialist teams

XYRA is positioned as the first-pass engineering assistant: it accelerates extraction, makes uncertainty visible, and keeps the engineer in control.

## Slide 3 - Deliverables

Engineering deliverables, not a chatbot demo.

- Instrument Index
- IO List
- Piping MTO
- Verification Log and QA evidence
- PrecisionPDF Review

## Slide 4 - Deployment

Runs where the drawings live.

- One exposed port for browser access.
- Backend, worker, Redis, and model runtime stay inside Docker networks.
- Client drawings and extracted data remain local.
- Project legend and naming rules can guide extraction.
- Health dashboard and support-bundle roadmap reduce support burden.

## Slide 5 - Pilot Offer

Prove value on a real drawing package.

Week 1:
- Install inside client network.
- Load project legend and sample drawings.
- Run first extraction and review calibration.

Week 2:
- Process 20-50 representative P&IDs.
- Compare against manual workflow.
- Review Instrument Index, IO List, MTO, and PDF markups.

Decision pack:
- Coverage summary.
- Review exceptions.
- Deployment checklist.
- Monthly license proposal.
